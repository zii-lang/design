We're excited to announce that the **Z Programming Language Design Documentation** is now live!

We've set up this documentation website using [Docusaurus](https://docusaurus.io/) to provide a central place for documenting the ideas, design decisions, and architectural foundations behind the Z programming language.

The purpose of this repository is to capture design ideas as they emerge, explore different approaches, and establish a clear architectural direction before putting them into practice.

### Design Documents vs. RFCs

It's important to distinguish these documents from RFCs.

Design documents focus on exploring and documenting the language's architecture, internal mechanisms, and design decisions. They serve as a reference throughout development and help us maintain a consistent understanding of how different parts of the language are intended to work.

RFCs, on the other hand, will have a separate process and repository. Once the compiler reaches a more mature and complete state, we'll introduce an RFC process for proposing, discussing, and reviewing new language features and significant changes.

### What's Next?

For now, this repository will serve as the foundation for documenting Z's architecture and design. As development progresses, we'll continue expanding the documentation to cover more aspects of the language, its compiler, and its underlying infrastructure.

We believe that documenting these decisions early will make the project easier to maintain, understand, and evolve over time.

**Stay tuned for more updates as Z continues to take shape!**
